Never miss the strike again.

Peguei keeps the camera always recording and only keeps the last few seconds, without filling up your phone. When the fish strikes, tap the screen: the saved video already starts BEFORE your tap, with the whole strike and with sound.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open Peguei and lean the phone toward your rod or the water.
• The buffer runs on its own. Nothing to press while you wait.
• Fish on? Tap the button. The clip joins the seconds before with everything you record until you tap again.
• Done: the video stays in the app's gallery to watch, export and share.

UP TO 60 SECONDS OF BUFFER
Choose how much is kept before the tap: 15 to 60 seconds. 720p, 1080p or 4K at 24, 30 or 60 fps, depending on what your phone's camera supports.

0.5X LENS FOR THE WHOLE SCENE
On phones with an ultra-wide camera, use 0.5x to frame the boat, the rod and the fish coming out of the water. 1x, 2x and pinch-to-zoom work too.

READY FOR REELS AND TIKTOK
Export a vertical 9:16 copy with the species and weight in the corner and share it straight to WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok or YouTube. The original video stays as it was.

BUILT FOR A DAY BY THE WATER
• Pocket mode: black screen to save battery while the buffer keeps recording.
• Volume buttons or a Bluetooth selfie remote start and stop recording without touching the screen.
• Optional automatic buffer shutdown after a while without use.

YOUR VIDEOS STAY WITH YOU
Peguei records on your device. Videos never go to our servers: you decide what to share. No login and no cross-app tracking. The app sends usage, crash and performance data so we can improve Peguei, and you can turn it off in Settings → Privacy.

FREE, NO SUBSCRIPTION
Peguei is free, with no subscription and no on-screen ads. When Peguei has a sponsor, exported videos may carry the sponsor's mark, and the app tells you before you export.

Also great for hiking, surfing, cycling and any moment that happens too fast to start recording from scratch.
