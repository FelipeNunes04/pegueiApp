Privacy Policy — Peguei

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Peguei app ("the App") handles user information.

1. Who we are

Peguei is developed by Felipe Nunes. For privacy questions, contact us at felipennunes04@icloud.com.

2. What data the App accesses

Peguei is designed to work entirely on your device, without relying on a server for its core function. The App accesses:

  • Camera: used to show the live preview and continuously record the video buffer (the sliding window of seconds that lets you save what already happened before you tap the button).
  • Microphone: used to record the audio track included with saved clips.
  • Storage / Photos: used to save the final video clips to your device.

3. What the App does NOT do

  • It does not send video, audio, or the camera preview to any server. All recording, buffering, and clip saving happen locally, on your device.
  • It does not use third-party services to process audio or voice. Captured audio is only recorded into the saved clip's own sound track; the App has no voice-command or speech-recognition feature in this version.
  • It does not display ads.
  • It has no account, login, or personal data collection (name, email, phone number) for the App to function.

2.1 Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics (a Google service) to understand, in aggregate, how the App is used. This helps us prioritize improvements and show partners and sponsors aggregate usage numbers (for example, how many videos carrying a sponsor's watermark were exported). What is collected:

  • Screen views (which screen of the App was opened).
  • Usage events, never the video content: buffer started and stopped (duration and lens), recording started, video saved (duration, resolution, frame rate and lens), recording failures (error code, no file names), video exported (with or without watermark and caption), which app a video was shared to (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook or other), review prompt shown, and whether the donation Pix key was copied.
  • Optional onboarding answers: kind of fishing and Brazilian state. You can skip this step.
  • Exported video caption: the species name you type and whether you entered a weight (the weight value itself is not sent).
  • Fishing sessions and device state: how long the buffer ran, how many videos were saved in the session, battery level, the device's thermal state (nominal, fair, serious or critical), memory used by the App, dropped frames and the connection type (Wi-Fi, cellular or offline). On Android the free storage range is also sent (for example, "2 to 5 GB"); on iPhone free storage is only used on the device, to warn you when it is running out.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports through Firebase Crashlytics, and camera start, save and export times through Firebase Performance Monitoring. Reports include the recording settings (buffer length, lens, resolution) and the thermal state, never videos or file names.
  • Pesca Certa integration: if Pesca Certa opens Peguei, or you send a catch to the Pesca Certa diary, we record that it happened and the species sent. The link carries only the species, the date and, when known, an approximate area (a geohash of about 5 km), never the exact spot.
  • Standard Firebase technical identifiers (app install ID, device model, OS version and app version). We do not collect name, email, phone number, device location or the advertising identifier (Ad ID).

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Send usage data. The choice is stored only on your device.

This data is processed by Google as a processor, under Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase's Terms. No video, audio or images are ever sent, only the events and metadata described above.

2.2 Sponsorship

When a sponsor is active, the App downloads the sponsor's brand image (via Firebase Remote Config and an https address) and applies it on your device to the videos you export. Your videos are never sent to the sponsor or to us.

4. Where your data is stored

Video clips you save are stored locally, on your own device's storage (in the operating system's media folder). Peguei does not keep a copy of these files on any server of its own or a third party's. If you delete a clip from the App or uninstall the App, the files stop existing (subject to your operating system's standard app-uninstall behavior).

5. Operating system permissions

The App requests the following permissions, always with an in-app explanation shown before the system prompt:

PermissionWhy it's needed
CameraRecording the live preview and the continuous video buffer
MicrophoneRecording the audio track included with saved clips
Storage / PhotosSaving the final video clips to the device

You can revoke any permission at any time from your operating system's settings. Without camera permission, the App cannot perform its core function.

6. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA, LGPD, and equivalent laws)

Because Peguei does not collect identifiable personal data on a server of its own (the only data sent to a server is the aggregated analytics events described in Section 2.1), most rights guaranteed by privacy laws (access, correction, deletion, portability) can already be exercised directly by you, on your own device: clips and settings are under your local control. If you have any privacy-related question or request — including about analytics data — contact us using the email in Section 1.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you may have additional rights under the GDPR or CCPA, respectively — contact us to exercise them. If you are located in Brazil, you have rights under the LGPD (access, correction, deletion, portability, and others) — the same contact channel applies.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. If the App ever adds analytics, voice processing, or any other feature that changes the data handling described here, this policy will be updated before that change takes effect.
